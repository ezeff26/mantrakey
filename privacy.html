Privacy Policy

MantraKey (Android) · Effective October 23, 2026

In short: MantraKey does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect the text you type, passwords, personal information, location, or device identifiers. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. No internet access

MantraKey does not request the INTERNET permission, so it has no way to send anything you type off your phone. All input is processed entirely on your device.

3. What is stored on your device only

  • Favorite phrases that you add yourself
  • App settings, such as whether you have finished the tutorial
  • An error log used to diagnose crashes (it does not contain the text you type)

This data stays on your phone and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you have turned on Android's Google backup, the Android system may back up these settings to your own Google account. That backup is managed by you and Google, and we cannot access it.

4. About Android's keyboard warning

When you enable a new keyboard, Android warns that it "may be able to collect all the text you type." Android shows this standard message for every keyboard app. MantraKey does not store or transmit the text you type.

5. Children

Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and revise the effective date.
